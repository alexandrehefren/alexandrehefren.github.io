## Research Interests

My research sits at the intersection of physics, quantitative biology, and data science. I am interested in using mathematical and computational methods to tackle problems in genomics. As a postdoctoral researcher in single-molecule genomics and bioinformatics at the University of Exeter, I develop computational and quantitative approaches to analyse high-resolution genomic data and better understand chromatin organisation.

My academic work spans theoretical physics and the application of quantitative methods to biological and genomic problems.

Institutional profile: [University of Exeter](https://experts.exeter.ac.uk/43233-alexandre-hefren-de-vasconcelos).
