Named after a record I keep returning to — *Transit in Summer* — for the feeling
it gives of being in motion and in no hurry.

Everything here was made on a phone, on the way to somewhere else. Not for
convenience, but because it is the camera I have when I am not intending to
photograph anything. These frames are quicker and less deliberate than the rest
of the site. That is what they are for.
