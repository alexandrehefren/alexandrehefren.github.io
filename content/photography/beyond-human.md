The other inhabitants — birds, dogs, whatever was already there before I arrived
and stayed after I left.

I photograph them the way I photograph people: waiting for the light and the
gesture to agree. The difference is that nothing here is aware of being looked
at, or performing for it. That unselfconsciousness is most of what I am after.
