When I moved to the UK to pursue my PhD at York, I was excited to get my first DSLR camera to document the novel enviroment around me. In 2023, I upgraded to a 35 mm (full-frame) mirrorless from Canon, which is now my main camera. Photography is, for me, a way of attending to the world. I discovered enjoyment in photography from a desire to "capture" time and reflect on it later. Initially, I found myself taking photos primarily when visiting new places or during trips. As I also enjoy walking outdoors, street photography became a natural way for me to continue capturing these frozen moments in time, and a reason to keep looking.
